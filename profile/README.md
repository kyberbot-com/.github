<h1 align="center">KyberBot</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Your SI should work only for you.</strong></p>

KyberBot is the personal assistant that knows you best. Its memory is yours: kept on your own devices, encrypted, readable by nobody else. It lives in every KyberDeck form, from a card on a lanyard to a robot in the hallway, hands the heavy work to KyberAgent swarms, and asks you before anything sensitive.

## What it is

- **Your memory, your keys.** Kept on your devices, encrypted.
- **One person to answer to: you.** No second customer.
- **Asks before it acts** on anything sensitive.
- **One assistant, from a card to a robot**, running the rest through KyberAgent.
